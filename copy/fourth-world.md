## story.h2
Thirty years inside this music

## story.lede
I have been listening to Jon Hassell since high school. No musician
has shaped me more. The Fourth World idea, music that sounds like a
place that does not exist, has been the center of my listening life
since before I could name it.

## story.body
In the 2010s that influence became a working life. I joined Jon's
band and stayed for the decade, on stage with him through his last
concert in London in 2015, and in the studio for the two final
albums, Listening to Pictures and Seeing Through Sound. I recorded,
co-produced, and performed on both. That music was built at close
range, over years, in rooms Jon and I shared.

## story.body2
Jon is gone. The method is not. This record is my answer to the only
question worth asking after a loss like that: what happens to the
music now? It is heavily shaped by my relationship with him, and it
is made with the conviction the title states. A fourth world is
still possible.
