Lineage

I recorded and produced Jon Hassell's last two albums, and played in his band through the 2010s. This music comes out of that decade.

On stage

Solo ambient sets, played live: Chapman Stick, bass guitar, and electronics. Long tones, live processing, one performer.

The Fourth World project is being built for the stage as well, carrying Jon Hassell's live language forward. Promoters and festivals who want either set can reach me directly.
