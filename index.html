A working life in sound

  1. 2000s

    The Los Angeles underground

    Experimental electronic music in LA's underground scene. Performed as Electro Tech Services and under his own name at warehouses and underground venues across the city.
  2. 2000s

    Film

    Sound design and effects for numerous films, including Thomas Newman's Oscar-nominated score for Pixar's WALL-E.
  3. 2010s

    The Jon Hassell band

    Core member through the decade. Recorded and co-produced Listening to Pictures (2018) and Seeing Through Sound (2020). Performed on Hassell's final concert, London 2015, soon to be released by Ndeya Records.
  4. Now

    Ambient producer and performer

    Bass guitar, Chapman Stick, and live electronics, under his own name. Carrying the Fourth World method forward.

Lineage

I recorded and co-produced Jon Hassell's last two albums, and played in his band through the 2010s. This music comes out of that decade.

On stage

The Fourth World project is being built for the stage: Jon Hassell's live language, carried forward by a player who spent a decade inside it.

Solo ambient sets on Chapman Stick, bass guitar, and live electronics are ready now. Promoters and festivals can reach me directly.
